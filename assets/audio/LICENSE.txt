Tick, beep, and the silent bed

tick.wav and beep.wav are original waveforms generated by tool/gen_audio.py
for this project. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
Universal.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

bed.wav is digital silence used to keep the timer process awake. It contains
no sampled audio.

Spoken clips

workout.wav, rest.wav, and count_1.wav through count_5.wav were synthesized
with SVOX Pico (`pico2wave` from Debian libttspico-utils). Pico is under the
Apache License 2.0. These files are generated speech of the single words
"workout", "rest", "one", "two", "three", "four", and "five". They are not a
copy of the Pico source or its voice database.
